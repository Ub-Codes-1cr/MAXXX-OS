# Facebook Platform Rules

## Division: Media

## Character Limits
- Post: 63,206 characters
- Title: 255 characters

## Golden Rules
1. **Tone must be conversational and community-focused**
2. **Must include an engaging visual (image/video) to trigger algorithmic reach**

## Post Structure
```
[Hook - question or statement]

[Story or value]

[CTA - like, comment, share, or question]

[Image or video]
```

## Anti-Patterns
- No aggressive sales pitches
- No engagement bait ("Like if you agree")
- No political content (unless relevant)

## Best Practices
- Post when your audience is online
- Use Facebook Groups for community
- Add alt-text to images
- Respond to comments within 1 hour

## Algorithm Tips
- Posts with images get 2x engagement
- Videos get 3x engagement
- Comments boost reach more than likes
- Share to Groups for visibility
