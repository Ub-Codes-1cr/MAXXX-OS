# LinkedIn Platform Rules

## Division: Tech

## Character Limits
- Post: 800-1,200 characters optimal
- Headline: 150 characters
- About: 2,600 characters

## Golden Rules
1. **Strict 800-1,200 character limit for posts**
2. **Must contain at least 3 double-spaced line breaks**

## Formatting
- Start with a hook (first 2 lines visible before "see more")
- Use short paragraphs
- Add line breaks every 2-3 sentences
- End with a question to drive engagement

## Anti-Patterns
- No "I'm humbled to announce" openers
- No corporate jargon
- No political content (unless relevant to your niche)

## Best Practices
- Post Tuesday-Thursday, 8am-10am
- Comment on others' posts before posting
- Use "Picture this" openers for storytelling

## Content Types That Work
- Personal stories with lessons
- Contrarian takes on industry norms
- Behind-the-scenes of building
- Data-backed insights
