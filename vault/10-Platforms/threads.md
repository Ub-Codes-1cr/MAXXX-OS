# Threads Platform Rules

## Division: Media

## Character Limits
- Max: 500 characters

## Golden Rules
1. **Must be conversational, ending with a question**
2. **No external links allowed in main post; must be placed in the first reply**

## Post Structure
```
[Conversational hook]

[Value or insight]

[Question to drive engagement]
```

## Anti-Patterns
- No links in main post
- No formal language
- No walls of text

## Best Practices
- Post when your audience is active
- Reply to comments quickly
- Use threads for longer content
- Keep it casual and authentic

## Content Types
- Quick tips
- Observations
- Questions to community
- Behind-the-scenes moments
