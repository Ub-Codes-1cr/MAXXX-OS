# LinkedOut Platform Rules

## Division: Media

## Character Limits
- Post: 3,000 characters

## Golden Rules
1. **Must contain a paradoxical career insight or satirical humor**
2. **Must use a specific narrative structure (Setup -> Twist -> Punchline)**

## Narrative Structure
```
[Setup - The conventional wisdom or situation]

[Twist - The unexpected reality or counterintuitive truth]

[Punchline - The memorable takeaway or lesson]
```

## Example
```
Everyone says you need a CS degree to be a great developer.

I dropped out of college and learned to code from YouTube tutorials.

Now I'm a Staff Engineer at a FAANG company.

The degree didn't make me a developer. The 10,000 hours of debugging did.
```

## Anti-Patterns
- No corporate jargon
- No humble brags
- No generic motivational quotes

## Best Practices
- Post when you have a genuine insight
- Use line breaks for readability
- End with a question to drive engagement
- Be authentic, not performative
