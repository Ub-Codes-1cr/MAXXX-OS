# X (Twitter) Platform Rules

## Division: Tech

## Character Limits
- Max: 280 characters per tweet
- Thread: Each tweet independent 280 limit

## Golden Rules
1. **No raw URLs in first tweet** - Place URLs in subsequent tweets of a thread
2. **Must start with contrarian hook or strong data point**

## Formatting
- Use line breaks for readability
- One idea per tweet in threads
- End threads with a CTA or question

## Anti-Patterns
- No engagement bait ("like if you agree")
- No vague posts without substance
- No excessive hashtags (max 2-3)

## Best Practices
- Post at peak hours (9am, 12pm, 5pm)
- Engage with replies within 1 hour
- Quote tweet your own threads for visibility

## Algorithm Tips
- First 30 minutes determine reach
- Replies boost visibility more than likes
- Threads get 2x engagement vs single tweets
