# Instagram Platform Rules

## Division: Media

## Character Limits
- Caption: 2,200 characters
- Hashtags: 30 max (5-10 recommended)

## Golden Rules
1. **Caption must end with a block of 5-10 relevant hashtags**
2. **First line must be a "punchy" hook under 50 characters**

## Caption Structure
```
[Hook - under 50 chars]

[Value - 2-3 sentences]

[CTA - question or invitation]

#hashtag1 #hashtag2 #hashtag3 #hashtag4 #hashtag5
```

## Anti-Patterns
- No walls of text
- No irrelevant hashtags
- No sales-heavy captions

## Best Practices
- Post when your audience is active (check Insights)
- Use carousel posts for 2x engagement
- Add location tags
- Reply to comments within 1 hour

## Hashtag Strategy
- Mix of small (10k), medium (100k), and large (1M) hashtags
- Use niche-specific hashtags
- Create a branded hashtag
