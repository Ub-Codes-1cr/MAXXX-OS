# Medium Platform Rules

## Division: Media

## Character Limits
- No strict limit, but 500-1,500 words optimal

## Golden Rules
1. **Paragraphs must not exceed 4 sentences**
2. **Sub-headers required every 150 words**
3. **Must include a high-quality featured image with alt-text**

## Formatting
- Use subtitles to break up content
- Add images every 300 words
- Use pull quotes for emphasis
- Add "Written by" bio at the end

## Anti-Patterns
- No walls of text
- No external links in first paragraph
- No passive voice-heavy writing

## Best Practices
- Publish Tuesday-Thursday, 7am-9am
- Apply for publications in your niche
- Use "Friend link" for paywall bypass
- Add alt-text to all images

## Monetization Tips
- Join the Partner Program
- Write for large publications
- Focus on engagement, not views
