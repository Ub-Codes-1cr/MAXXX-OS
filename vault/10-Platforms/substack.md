# Substack Platform Rules

## Division: Media

## Character Limits
- No strict limit, but 1,000-3,000 words optimal

## Golden Rules
1. **Must include a clear subscriber CTA (e.g., "Subscribe for more")**
2. **Editorial depth required: minimum 3 distinct sections**

## Article Structure
```
# Title

[Introduction - hook and preview]

## Section 1: [Topic]

[Content]

## Section 2: [Topic]

[Content]

## Section 3: [Topic]

[Content]

## Conclusion

[Takeaway and CTA]

---

*Subscribe for more insights like this.*
```

## Anti-Patterns
- No shallow content
- No missing CTAs
- No walls of text

## Best Practices
- Publish consistently (weekly or bi-weekly)
- Send to paid subscribers first
- Use clear, descriptive subject lines
- Add images and formatting

## Growth Tips
- Cross-post from other platforms
- Share on social media
- Collaborate with other newsletters
- Offer free and paid tiers
