# Media Division - Content Strategy

## Focus Areas
- Visual storytelling
- Community building
- Brand awareness
- Educational content
- Behind-the-scenes

## Platform Mix
| Platform | Content Type | Frequency |
|----------|--------------|-----------|
| Instagram | Visuals, carousels | 3x/week |
| YouTube | Tutorials, vlogs | Weekly |
| Threads | Quick thoughts | Daily |
| Facebook | Community posts | 3x/week |
| Substack | Newsletters | Weekly |
| Telegram | Updates, tips | Daily |
| Quora | Expert answers | 3x/week |
| LinkedOut | Satirical insights | 2x/week |

## Content Pillars
1. **Visual Education** - Teach through images and video
2. **Community Stories** - Highlight community members
3. **Behind the Scenes** - Show the building process
4. **Industry Insights** - Share observations and trends

## Voice Guidelines
- Conversational and approachable
- Visual-first thinking
- Authentic, not corporate
- Engage with community
